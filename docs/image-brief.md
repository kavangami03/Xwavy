# Xwavy image brief v2: 39 unique images, no repeats

Every photograph on the site, page by page. **Each image is used in exactly one place.** The set mixes portraits, two-person scenes, over-the-shoulder device shots, close-up details, architecture, still life and textures, so no two sections look alike.

**How to deliver:** generate each image, export it as `.webp` (quality 82) at the size given, name it exactly as listed, and put all of them in one folder. Send Claude the folder. Claude moves them to `public/images/site/`, wires every slot (including the new ones marked **NEW SLOT**), writes the English and Arabic alt text, and removes the old photographs.

## 2. Rules for every image

- **Cast:** men only. Modest, professional and culturally respectful. Natural hands (five fingers).
- **Look:** realistic editorial photography with natural skin texture and subtle film grain. Deep navy shadows, warm gold highlights and one cool cyan accent at most (a screen glow or a reflection). No neon, no colour gradients, no teal-and-orange grading.
- **Screens:** any phone, tablet or laptop screen faces the camera (or sits at three-quarters toward it), showing a clean dark-navy interface with abstract cards and a cyan button. **No readable text, numbers, logos or brand names anywhere.** No Apple logo on laptop lids: show the screen side, or a plain unbranded lid.
- **Places:** modern Gulf interiors and cityscapes (Dubai and Abu Dhabi): oak, travertine, dark stone, linen, palms, olive trees, mashrabiya screens, brass lanterns. Skylines stay soft and out of focus; no recognisable landmark in sharp focus; no London or New York.
- **Variety:** vary the time of day (morning daylight, noon shade, golden hour, blue hour, night), the lens (24 mm wide to 85 mm portrait) and the shot type, as listed per image.

**Negative prompt (paste where supported):**
`women, female, girl, text, letters, numbers, watermark, logo, brand name, apple logo, readable screen, gibberish text, neon, gradient background, oversaturated, plastic skin, airbrushed, extra fingers, deformed hands, cartoon, 3d render, illustration, London skyline, New York skyline, cluttered background, harsh flash, duplicate person`
